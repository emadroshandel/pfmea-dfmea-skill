# Mechanical Assembly Template

For DFMEAs on mechanical components and assemblies (gears, bearings, shafts, housings, fasteners) and PFMEAs on the machining, forming, and assembly processes that produce them (press-fit, welding, torque/fastening, machining).

Typical failure modes: fracture, wear, deformation, loosening, misalignment, dimensional non-conformance. Typical PFMEA process steps: cleaning, welding/joining, heat treatment, machining, press-fit assembly, torque/fastening, final inspection.

Common noise factors: load cycling, vibration, temperature extremes, lubrication breakdown, manufacturing tolerance stack-up.
