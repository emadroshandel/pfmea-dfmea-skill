# Electronic / Electrical Template

For DFMEAs on electronic control units, sensors, wiring harnesses, PCBAs, and connectors, and PFMEAs on the electronics assembly processes that build them (SMT, wave solder, conformal coating, connector crimping, functional test).

Typical failure modes: open/short circuit, intermittent connection, drift out of tolerance, thermal failure, EMC non-compliance, firmware/logic fault (for DFMEA where software is in scope). Typical PFMEA process steps: SMT placement, reflow, wave/selective solder, conformal coating, connector crimp/insertion, in-circuit test, functional test, final inspection.

Common noise factors for the P-Diagram: thermal cycling, vibration, humidity/condensation, voltage transients, EMI.
