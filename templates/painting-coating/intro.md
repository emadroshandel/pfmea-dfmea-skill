# Painting / Coating Template

For PFMEAs on paint, e-coat, and powder-coating processes. As with surface treatment, this is almost always a PFMEA concern rather than a DFMEA one.

Typical failure modes: uneven texture/orange peel, color mismatch, poor adhesion, insufficient film thickness, contamination (dirt/fisheyes), corrosion after coating failure. Typical process steps: pretreatment/cleaning, primer application, base coat, clear coat, cure/bake, final inspection.

Common causes cluster around booth environment (temperature/humidity/particulate), substrate preparation, and application parameters (film thickness, cure temperature/time).
