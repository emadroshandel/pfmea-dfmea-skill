# Surface Treatment Template

For PFMEAs on plating, heat treatment, anodizing, and related surface-finishing processes. (These processes are almost always analyzed as PFMEA — the "design" of a plating spec belongs to the part's DFMEA, not this template.)

Typical failure modes: insufficient/excessive coating thickness, poor adhesion, hardness out of specification, distortion from thermal processing, contamination, corrosion. Typical process steps: cleaning/degreasing, surface activation, plating/coating bath, rinse, drying, heat treatment (quench/temper), post-process inspection.

Common causes cluster around bath chemistry drift, temperature/time control, and fixture/racking issues.
